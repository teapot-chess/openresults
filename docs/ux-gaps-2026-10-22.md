# UX gap audit, 2026-10-22

The same walk Alnasl had on 2026-10-21, on the public results site. Every
route in `router.ex` was read for what a reader expects of it: a way back
from every dead end, a sentence instead of a blank or raw text when
something fails, feedback after every form, a page that says when it has
stopped updating, labels and focus, Dutch in "je", and help text that still
matches the product decisions ("live results" are not "live boards"; no norm
indicators; no round times).

Read alongside `docs/accessibility-2026-09-13.md` and
`docs/browser-review-2026-09-12.md`; nothing fixed there is re-reported
here. Three of their open recommendations were small enough to close in this
pass and are in the table below. Code was read; no browser was driven.

## Fixed in this pass

| Page | Gap | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Any unknown address; any crash | The generator's "Not Found" / "Internal Server Error": two bare words on an unstyled white page, no way back. What a mistyped link shared in a club chat lands on. | medium | `ErrorHTML` renders a styled page in the reader's language (from `Accept-Language`): a heading, a sentence, "Go to the front page". The admin gate's 404 is still byte-for-byte the unknown-route page (tests compare the two) |
| Live boards (game, all boards, projector) | When the socket dropped the boards froze and the live dot kept beating; nothing said the moves had stopped | medium | "Reconnecting - moves are paused" appears on disconnect and goes on reconnect (`LiveBoardsComponents.offline_note/1`, inside an always-present status region); the live dot stops beating on a disconnected view |
| Entry form, terms, report form, front page search, field errors (Dutch) | 49 Dutch strings said "u"/"uw"; the rest of the site and the other apps say "je" | medium | All converted, verb inversions included ("Wil je", "Sta je", "hoor je"); `DutchRegisterTest` fails on any "u"/"uw" in a Dutch `msgstr` |
| Live boards, game and all-boards view, unknown round | "This round has not been published." and nothing else: a dead end from an old or hand-typed link | low | A "Go to the latest round" link under it when any round is published |
| Entry form, FIDE search | Without JavaScript the search box took typing and answered nothing (browser review 2026-09-12, #3) | low | Hidden until `html.has-js`, like every other script-only control |
| Entry form, Federation | `autocomplete="country"` made browsers offer "BE" to a field that wants "BEL" (accessibility pass, R3) | low | `autocomplete="off"`, with a comment saying why |
| Night, Slate, Ultra themes | Native dropdown lists, scrollbars and checkboxes drawn light on a dark page (accessibility pass, R2) | low | `color-scheme` per theme block; the default follows the system |

## Checked and fine

Front page (empty state, "no tournaments match your search"), tournament
pages for a slug that never published or was taken down (own 404 with a way
back), unknown round / team / player numbers (own 404s with a back link),
player history and event pages (404 with a sentence), the category filter
(present on standings, cross-table and pairings, offered whenever the
tournament publishes categories, removable as a chip, matches every category
a player carries), entry form (refusals say what to fix and keep the
answers, closed / full / not-yet-open each have their own sentence, the
success page says what happens next and that duplicates are the organisers'
job), report form (says who reads it and that nothing changes until then),
terms page, changelog, hall display (already says "Reconnecting…"),
results refresher (says "not updating - connection lost"), admin panel
(every destructive action has a confirmation page and a flash), gettext
(no fuzzy entries, no empty `msgstr` in nl or fr), help and page text (no
mention of norms or round times; "Live boards" is used only for the
relay-fed boards pages, never for the results).

## Needs a decision

| Page | Gap | Options |
|---|---|---|
| Standings, cross-table, pairings | The refresher rewrites the page every 20 s with no way to stop it; WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide) arguably applies, and a screen reader's browse cursor still resets (accessibility pass, R1) | (a) a "Pause live updates" button in the footer, remembered per tab (small); (b) also morph instead of replace (medium); (c) leave: live results are the point |
| Entry form | A player cannot change or withdraw an entry they sent; the page tells them to send it again or write to the organisers | (a) a private edit/withdraw link on the success page (needs a secret per entry, and a way for the change to reach OpenPairings' queue: a contract change); (b) leave, as the form says ("the same as a paper form") |
| Category filter | Offered only when the publisher sends `tournament.categories` (the arbiter's "Categories" display setting, newer OpenPairings). An older publisher's tournament, or one with categories hidden, has no Category control even when standings show a category column | (a) fall back to the distinct `players[].category` values when `tournament.categories` is absent (no contract change; but it would show categories the arbiter chose to hide); (b) as now |
| Report form | A reporter who leaves no address hears nothing ever again, and the page cannot show what became of a report | (a) a reference number on the outcome page that the operator's resolution can be looked up by; (b) as now (the page says so plainly) |
